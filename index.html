Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is the house?

24 Society Salon & Wellness, E3A, Shri Rangam, New Colony, Panch Batti, MI Road, Jaipur 302001.

When are you open?

Monday to Sunday, 09:00 to 21:00, by reservation.

How do I reserve?

Call +91 92510 49696 or +91 92510 49695, write to jaipurdesk@24societysalonandwellness.com, or use the enquiry form below. Every enquiry is answered by a member of the house.

Are the prices inclusive of GST?

All prices are in INR and exclusive of GST, with terms and conditions applying. Hair is priced by stylist level; every other service is single-priced.

How does bridal work?

Bridal is a calendar, not a package: consultation, a skin plan, a hair plan, trial days, movement and recovery, the wedding morning and event looks. Bridal hair and makeup is ₹30,000 at the salon and ₹65,000 at your venue.

Can the Café be booked privately?

Yes. The 24 Society Café can be reserved for private experiences. Contact the house for details.

How do I join as a member?

Membership is by invitation or by choice. Tier names, pricing and the full benefit matrix are refined for each member on enquiry.
